# 2000 Exam 9 - Q34

**Points:** 2

## Question

Answer the following based on Mahler's "An Example of Credibility and Shifting Risk Parameters."

### Part a (1.50 pts)

Briefly describe three criteria used to compare the performance of credibility methods.

### Part b (0.50 pts)

Mahler states that one criterion differs from the other two criteria on a conceptual level. Which criterion is that? Briefly state in what way it differs from the others.

## Solution

### Part a

i. Least squared error: minimize the squared error between the actual and predicted results

ii. Limited fluctuation (aka small chance of large errors): minimize the likelihood that any one

actual observation will be a certain percent different from the predicted result

iii. Meyers/Dorweiler: minimize the correlation between predictions and prediction errors

### Part b

Meyers/Dorweiler is different from the other 2 criteria since it focuses on the pattern of the errors instead of minimizing prediction errors.
