# 2010 Exam 9 - Q23

**Points:** 3.25

## Question

### Part a (1.25 pts)

Use the following information to calculate experience modification factors for each insured:

Losses Used for Experience Rating

| Insured | Losses | Expected losses | Credibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V | $192,500 | $175,000 | 45% |
| W | $350,000 | $375,000 | 50% |
| X | $450,000 | $425,000 | 55% |
| Y | $162,500 | $165,000 | 40% |
| Z | $120,000 | $100,000 | 35% |

### Part b (2.00 pts)

Use the information below to discuss whether the factors calculated in part (a) above appear to be more equitable than the current modification factors:

Subsequent Period

| Insured | Manual Premium | Losses | Current Modification Factors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V | $100,000 | $60,000 | 1.10 |
| W | $150,000 | $95,000 | 0.95 |
| X | $250,000 | $150,000 | 1.05 |
| Y | $80,000 | $50,000 | 1.03 |
| Z | $50,000 | $42,000 | 1.00 |

## Solution

### Part a

Here we can calculate the mods using Mod = [ZA + (1-Z)E]/E.

Mod

1.04 — `=(E10*C10+(1-E10)*D10)/D10`

0.97 — `=(E11*C11+(1-E11)*D11)/D11`

1.03 — `=(E12*C12+(1-E12)*D12)/D12`

0.99 — `=(E13*C13+(1-E13)*D13)/D13`

1.07 — `=(E14*C14+(1-E14)*D14)/D14`

### Part b

Since we don't care here about whether too much or too little credibility is applied, we don't really have to sort the mods in order to answer this question. All we really care about here is the variance in standard loss ratios under each plan. Really, we should only look at the variances for grouped risks, so it would also have been reasonable if you attempted to group the risks into 3 groups before calculating and comparing variances.

Std LR with curr mod

0.545 — `=D22/(E22*C22)`

0.667 — `=D23/(E23*C23)`

0.571 — `=D24/(E24*C24)`

0.607 — `=D25/(E25*C25)`

0.840 — `=D26/(E26*C26)`

**Variance::** 0.014 — `=VAR.S(J22:J26)`

Since the standard loss ratios show less variance under the part (a) mods, the part (a) mods are more equitable than the current mods.

Std LR with part (a) mod

0.574 — `=D22/(I10*C22)`

0.655 — `=D23/(I11*C23)`

0.581 — `=D24/(I12*C24)`

0.629 — `=D25/(I13*C25)`

0.785 — `=D26/(I14*C26)`

0.007 — `=VAR.S(M22:M26)`
