# 2010 Exam 5 - Q34

**Points:** 1

## Question

An insurer has been tracking the claims experience of a very large construction company for the three years the construction company has been insured by this insurer. The construction company will implement a new safety program starting in the upcoming year.

### Part a (0.50 pts)

Determine whether the insurer should use experience rating, schedule rating, or both to rate the construction company for the upcoming policy period. Briefly explain your answer.

### Part b (0.50 pts)

Assuming no additional changes, determine whether the insurer should use experience rating, schedule rating, or both to rate the construction company five years from now. Briefly explain your answer.

## Solution

### Part a

The insurer should use both experience rating and schedule rating for the upcoming policy period. Experience rating will reflect the actual experience over the last few years, and schedule rating will reflect the expected impact of the new safety program.

### Part b

Depending on whether the insured's experience is fully credible, you could answer this different ways.

Solution 1: Insured's experience is fully credible

The insurer should only use experience rating. Since the safety program would now be fully reflected in the insured's experience, the experience rating will pick up the impact of the safety program. Using schedule rating to reflect the safety program would double count the impact of the program.

Solution 2: Insured's experience is NOT fully credible

The insurer can use experience rating and schedule rating if the insured's experience is not fully credible. While the safety program will be reflected in the full experience period, if the experience is not given full credibility, then some partial schedule credit for the safety program could still be appropriate depending on the benefits of the program and the credibility given to the insured's experience.
