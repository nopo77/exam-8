By "equal premium size" the question is referring to manual premium, since the mods will cause the standard premium to differ between risks (that's the point of experience rating). The data is already sorted by the Plan A mod.

| 1,2 | 63.5% | 81.9% | 1,4 | 65.0% | 89.0% | The wtd avg Standard LR for a quintile = Sum(Losses) / Sum(Std Prem). |

Even though the question specifically says "Quintiles Test," the graders also accepted the Efficiency Test (since it still involves grouping data into quintiles).

Solution 1: Quintiles Test

Plan A and B have similar dispersion in manual LRs, with plan A having a slightly greater range (63.5% to 89% vs. 65% to 89%), so plan A is slightly better at identifying risk differences. The trend is standard LRs is not as clear, though it appears both plans have a downward trend, suggesting that both plans give too much credibility to experience. Plan B seems to have a more negative trend due to the high standard LR for the first quintile, and as such plan A seems to be the better performing plan.
