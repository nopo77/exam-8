# Practice Problem 7 - Fisher Chapter 1 Q5

## Question

Discuss the concepts of process variance and the variance of the hypothetical means (VHM) and how they relate to experience rating.

## Solution

In a group of risks, some of the difference in experience is due to underlying differences in the loss potential of the different risks. This is the variance of the hypothetical means. Some of the difference in experience among the risks is purely random, i.e., the process variance. Experience rating attempts to identify and adjust for the VHM, while at the same time not penalizing risks for differences in experience that are purely random.
