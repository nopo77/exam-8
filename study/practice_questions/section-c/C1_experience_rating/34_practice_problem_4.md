# Practice Problem 4 - Fisher Chapter 1 Q2

## Question

Explain how experience rating increases equity.

## Solution

Experience rating adjusts a risk's rate to be more in line with that risk's expected loss experience. Risks whose expected loss experience is lower than average will pay less premium, and risks whose expected loss experience is higher will pay more premium.
