# 2009 Exam 9 - Q20

**Points:** 1.5

## Question

Experience rating is an important aspect of individual risk rating.

### Part a (0.50 pts)

Briefly describe two primary goals of experience rating.

### Part b (0.50 pts)

With regard to experience rating, describe the relationship between the variance of the hypothetical means and the credibility of individual risk experience.

### Part c (0.50 pts)

Describe the actuarial concept of individual rate equity as it applies to experience rating.

## Solution

### Part a

Any 2 of:

i. Greater risk equity - The degree of the charge based on past experience should be the

degree to which it is predictive of future losses.

ii. Safety incentive - By charging insureds a higher premium for prior losses, insureds have a

financial incentive for loss control.

iii. Enhance market competition - Insurers will be willing to insure more risks since experience rating

helps guarantee an equal profit potential on all risks after the application of the experience mod.

### Part b

Solution 1: Mathematical answer

Z = E / (E+K) and K = EPV/VHM. As the VHM increases, K decreases, so the credibility of individual risk experience Z increases.

Solution 2: Explaining the logic

The higher the VHM, the higher the credibility assigned to individual experience. The more varied the individual risks are in a class, the more valuable the individual experience becomes in determining a proper individual rate.

### Part c

Individual rate equity means charging risks a rate that is commensurate with their expected losses.
