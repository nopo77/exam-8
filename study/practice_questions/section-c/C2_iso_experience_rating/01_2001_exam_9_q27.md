## Solution

Since we aren't told anything about any significant changes in exposures, we will use the standard method to calculate the CSLC. We are told the insured first had a claims-made policy in 1992. That would make the 1992 policy a first-year claims-made policy, 1993 a second-year claims-made policy, 1994 third-year, 1995 fourth-year, and 1996 and later mature claims-made policies. Since the experience period starts in 1998, all of the policies in the experience period and the prospective policy in 2002 will all be mature claims-made policies. Table 1 has factors larger than 1, and is analogous to Table 13B in the ISO manual. Table 2 is analogous to Table 13C in the ISO manual. I assume Table 1 relates to the type of policy being rated. I assume Table 2 relates to the type of policy in the experience period. Since all policies are claims-made, there is no expected development to add since we set that to 0 for claims-made policy terms.
