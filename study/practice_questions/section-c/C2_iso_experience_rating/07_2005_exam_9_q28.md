# 2005 Exam 9 - Q28

**Points:** 3

## Question

Given the following information:

- Manufacturer's Inc. was formed in 1994.
- From 1994 until 2001, Manufacturer's Inc. purchased occurrence general liability policies.
- Effective January 1, 2002, Manufacturer's Inc. purchased its first claims-made general liability

policy with a retroactive date of January 1, 2002.

- Manufacturer's Inc. has purchased claims-made policies ever since.
- All Manufacturer's Inc. policies have been effective on January 1st.

For the policy being rated, the annual basic limits loss costs are:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| $100,000 | Premises/Operations |
| $200,000 | Products/Completed Operations |

Calculate the company subject loss cost (basic limits expected losses) for the January 1, 2005 general liability policy.

## Solution

The experience period is 1/1/2001 through 12/31/2003. The 2005 policy being rated is a 4th year claims-made policy (as the 2002 policy was a 1st year claims-made policy, the 2003 was a 2nd year claims-made policy). Lookup the PAFs and Detrend factors from the ISO manual.
