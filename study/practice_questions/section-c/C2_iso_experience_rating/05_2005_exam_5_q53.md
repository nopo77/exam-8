# 2005 Exam 5 - Q53

**Points:** 1

## Question

Answer the following questions regarding the ISO Commercial General Liability Experience and Schedule Rating Plan.

### Part a (0.50 pts)

Explain why the plan uses basic limits losses, and limits losses and allocated loss adjustment expenses to a maximum single loss limitation (MSL).

### Part b (0.50 pts)

Explain why the MSL increases as the size of the risk (as measured by premium or credibility) increases.

## Solution

### Part a

The limits prevent any large individual claims from overly influencing the experience mod. They also make the plan more responsive to the frequency of losses rather than the severity.

### Part b

The larger the risk, the larger expected losses, and the less impact a loss of a given size will have on the mod. As such, the MSL will increase to allow for more credibility to be given to the actual experience.
