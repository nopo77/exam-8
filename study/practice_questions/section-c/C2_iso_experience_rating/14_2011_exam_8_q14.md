# 2011 Exam 8 - Q14

**Points:** 1.5

## Question

An underwriter is rating an account containing general liability and workers compensation coverage.

Given the following:

- The insured has average schedule rating risk characteristics except for equipment, employees

and premises.

- All of the insured's equipment has been replaced within the last four months and all employees

have been trained on the new equipment.

- Five months ago, the insured cleaned its premises inside and installed new lighting outside to

improve visibility for its customers at night.

### Part a (1.00 pts)

Calculate the maximum schedule credit that can be applied to this account according to the ISO Commercial General Liability Experience and Schedule Rating Plan.

### Part b (0.50 pts)

The account has an overall debit mod for workers compensation. Briefly describe two conclusions that can be drawn from this.

## Solution

### Part a

The new equipment, the employee training, and the cleaning/new lighting all took place after the experience period for experience rating, so they would not be reflected in the experience mod. Therefore, it is appropriate to reflect them in the schedule mod.

From Rule 9 of the ISO manual:

The max credit for equipment is 10%. The max credit for premises is 10%. The max credit for employees is 6%.

The sum of the above is 26%, however, this is limited by the max credit of 25%. Therefore, the max total credit is 25%.

### Part b

The risk has worse than average experience for its class. The risk may not be a good fit for the class.
