## Solution

The experience period will be 1/1/2004 - 12/31/2006. All policies are occurrence, so the PAFs will be 1.00 for Tables 13B and 13C. For LDFs, we measure from the start of each policy term in the historical period to the evaluation date of 7/1/2007. As such, the appropriate LDFs are for 18 months (i.e., 1/1/2006 to 7/1/2007), 30 months, and 42 months.
