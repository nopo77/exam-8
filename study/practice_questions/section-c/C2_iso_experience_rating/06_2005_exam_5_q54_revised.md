# 2005 Exam 5 - Q54 revised

**Points:** 2

## Question

Given the following information for an insured, determine the General Liability premium after adjustments for experience and schedule rating. Show all work.

$75,800 — Manual premium

Experience rating information:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| $93,500 | Reported Basic Limits Losses and ALAE limited by MSL |
| $25,200 | Expected Unreported Limited Losses and ALAE |
| $153,900 | Company Subject Basic Limits Loss and ALAE Costs |
| 0.35 | Credibility |
| 0.92 | Expected Experience Ratio |

The underwriter has determined that the following schedule rating modifications are appropriate:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| 4% | Premises - Condition, Care |
| -7% | Equipment - Type, Condition, Care |
| -8% | Classification Peculiarities |
| 3% | Employees - Selection, Training, etc. |

## Solution

**AER:** 0.771 — `=(B11+B12)/B13`

Ex Mod — -0.057 — `=B14*(J3-B15)/B15` — Mod = Z * (AER - EER)/EER

**Sch Mod:** -8% — `=SUM(B19:B22)`

**Premium:** $65,790 — `=B7*(1+J5)*(1+J7)`
