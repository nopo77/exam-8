# 2009 Exam 9 - Q22

**Points:** 2

## Question

An insurance company has adopted the Insurance Services Office's Commercial General Liability Experience and Schedule Rating Plan. The following general liability schedule rating worksheet was completed for one of the company's insureds.

| Risk Characteristic | Credit | Debit | Comment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Location | - | 5% | Recent local lawsuits against businesses of this type |
| Premises | - | - |  |
| Equipment | 10% | - | Upgraded product testing equipment 5 months ago |
| Classification | - | - |  |
| Employees | 5% | - | Overhauled training program 3 years ago |
| Cooperation | 5% | - | Enacted safety procedures to protect customers 3 months ago |
| Total | 15% | - |  |

The following additional information is available:

- The insured is eligible for experience rating.
- The insured is up for its fourth renewal.
- Each policy has had a term of 12 months.

For each risk characteristic with a credit or debit applied, discuss the appropriateness of the modification.

## Solution

Location: This is not appropriate since the lawsuits are not related to location. Location is only for a change in exposures inside or outside the premises.

Equipment: This could be appropriate since the new equipment is not reflected in the experience period used for experience rating.

Employees: This is not appropriate, since this change is at least partially reflected in the experience period for experience rating. A smaller credit could potentially be appropriate if the program wasn't effective for the full experience period, or if the insured's experience isn't fully credible.

Cooperation: It would be appropriate to give a credit for a new safety program since it is not reflected in the experience period. However, the maximum credit for safety under cooperation is 2%, so giving a 5% credit would be inappropriate.
