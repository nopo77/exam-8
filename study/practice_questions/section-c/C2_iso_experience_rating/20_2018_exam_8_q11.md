# 2018 Exam 8 - Q11

**Points:** 1.25

## Question

Given the following information for a construction insured's general liability policy that is subject to the ISO Commercial General Liability Experience and Schedule Rating Plan:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| 270,500 | Company Subject Loss Cost |
| 0.85 | Actual Experience Ratio |
| 2020-01-01 | Policy Effective Date |
| Annual | Policy Term |

All major changes in the last five years for this insured:

- Upgraded all equipment in 2015
- Improved employee training in 2018
- Implemented a new safety program in 2018

### Part a (0.50 pts)

Calculate the experience modification for this policy.

### Part b (0.75 pts)

An underwriter has selected a credit of 10% for schedule rating. Assess the reasonability of this selection.

## Solution

### Part a

Lookup CSLC of 270,500 in Table 16 to get:

| L | M |
| --- | --- |
| Z | 0.43 |
| EER | 0.931 |

Mod — -3.7% — `=M3*(B8-M4)/M4` — Mod = Z * (AER - EER)/EER

### Part b

For a 2020 policy, the policy terms from 2016-2018 would make up the experience period. Furthermore, the insured has less than 100% credibility given to its experience, so even items fully reflected in the experience period might still warrant a schedule credit. We need to compare the credits given to the allowable credits for each category from the ISO manual.

The graders marked any credit to the equipment upgrade as incorrect since it is fully reflected in the experience period. I completely disagree with them, and so does the source text. For any doubts, see the last paragraph on page 8 of Fisher that continues onto page 9: "However, if the risk is too small to have fully credible experience, it might be appropriate to give some schedule credit …"

Equipment upgrade - fully reflected in experience period, but since experience has only 43% credibility, some credit may still be warranted. Max credit for this category is 10%, so perhaps a 3-5% credit could be warranted.

Employee training - Only reflected in 1 year of experience period (and has limited credibility). Max credit is 6%, so perhaps 5% or so credit is still warranted.

Safety program - Only reflected in 1 year of experience period (and has limited credibility). Max credit is 2%, so perhaps 1-2% or so credit is still warranted.

In total, the warranted credits total about 10%, so the underwriter's selected credit seems reasonable.
