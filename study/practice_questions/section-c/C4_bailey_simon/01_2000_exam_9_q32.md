# 2000 Exam 9 - Q32

**Points:** 3

## Question

Based on Bailey and Simon's "An Actuarial Note on the Credibility of Experience of a Single Private Passenger Car" and the table below, answer the following.

Class X - Two years claim free Class Y - One year claim free Class B - Zero years claim free

### Part a (1.50 pts)

Calculate the credibilities for a single private passenger car for one or more years, two or more years, and three or more years claim-free. Show all work.

### Part b (0.50 pts)

Briefly describe the relationship that Bailey and Simon expect between the three credibilities from part (a).

### Part c (1.00 pts)

Do the credibilities in part (a) follow the relationship described in part (b)? Briefly explain why or why not.
