# 2010 Exam 9 - Q5

**Points:** 1

## Question

An insurance company has a private passenger auto book of business with the following claims experience:

| Group | Number of Accident-Free Years | Earned Premium at Present Group D Rates | Number of Claims Incurred |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 3 or more | 60,000,000 | 45,000 |
| B | 2 | 15,000,000 | 15,000 |
| C | 1 | 20,000,000 | 29,300 |
| D | 0 | 5,000,000 | 18,700 |
|  |  | 100,000,000 | 108,000 |

Calculate the credibility of a single car for a driver with one or more accident-free years.

## Solution

**Mod:** 0.87037 — `=SUM(E8:E10)/SUM(D8:D10)/(E12/D12)`

**Z:** 0.130 — `=1-C17`
