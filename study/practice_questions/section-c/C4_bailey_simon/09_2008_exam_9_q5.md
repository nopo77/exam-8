# 2008 Exam 9 - Q5

**Points:** 2

## Question

A liability insurer collects the following data for a particular class of private passenger auto risks:

| Accident-Free Years | Earned Exposures | Incurred Losses ($) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 or more | 2,500 | 1,000,000 |
| 1 | 500 | 500,000 |
| 0 | 1,000 | 2,500,000 |
| Total | 4,000 | 4,000,000 |

Assume the following:

$1,250 — Base rate per exposure

- An experience rating factor is the only factor applied to the base rate.

### Part a (1.00 pts)

Calculate the credibility of an exposure that is accident-free for 1 or more years.

### Part b (1.00 pts)

Calculate the premium for an exposure that is accident-free for 2 or more years.

## Solution

### Part a

Note: in this problem, we cannot use relative frequency as we would for other problems to obtain the Mod since we don't have claim counts. As such, we have to use relative pure premium.

**Mod:** 0.5 — `=SUM(D7:D8)/SUM(C7:C8)/(D10/C10)`

**Credibility:** 0.5 — `=1-K5`

### Part b

**Mod:** 0.4 — `=(D7/C7)/(D10/C10)`

**Premium:** $500 — `=B14*K9`
