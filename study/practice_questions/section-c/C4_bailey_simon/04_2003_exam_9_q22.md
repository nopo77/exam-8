# 2003 Exam 9 - Q22

**Points:** 3

## Question

You are given the following data:

| Class | Years since last accident | Actual Earned Premium at Present B Rates | Earned Car Years | Number of Claims |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 3+ | 375,000 | 2,500 | 200 |
| X | 2 | 15,000 | 100 | 12 |
| Y | 1 | 22,500 | 150 | 20 |
| B | 0 | 37,500 | 250 | 38 |

Assume that the same rate is charged to all insureds within a class, and there have been no rate changes in or since the experience period.

### Part a (1.00 pts)

What is the credibility of 3 or more accident-free years of experience?

### Part b (1.00 pts)

What is the credibility of 1 or more accident-free years of experience?

### Part c (1.00 pts)

Give two possible reasons why the answer in part (a) is not 3 times the answer in part (b).
