# Practice Problem 7 - Fisher Chapter 2 Q11

## Question

Under what conditions is the risk transfer the same for a retrospective rating plan and a large deductible plan?

## Solution

The risk transfer is the same for a retrospective rating plan and a large deductible plan when:

a. The per-occurrence ratable loss limit for the retrospective rating plan equals the per-occurrence deductible.

b. The maximum ratable loss amount for the retrospective rating plan equals the aggregate deductible.

c. There is no minimum ratable loss amount for the retrospective rating plan.
