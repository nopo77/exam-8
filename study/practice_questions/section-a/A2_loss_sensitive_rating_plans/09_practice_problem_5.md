# Practice Problem 5

## Question

### Part a

Discuss the main differences between a large deductible plan and a self-insured retention.

### Part b

Which of these plans would you recommend to an insured that believes they are a good risk and thus can lower their premium costs, but still wants the insurer's service equivalent to a full-coverage policy?

## Solution

### Part a

The main differences are:

- Regulator approval may be required for self-insurance for mandatory coverages.
- The insured is responsible for handling small claims with self-insurance, while an insurer

handles all claims under a large deductible plan.

- The insurer is subject to credit risk on large deductible plans, since they need to be

reimbursed for deductible losses by the insured. No credit risk exists for self-insurance.

- Limits are generally reduced by deductibles but not retentions, so the plan specifications

can be slightly different for similar coverage.

### Part b

I would recommend a large deductible plan since they would reduce their premium for the high deductible, but the insurer would still handle all claims.
