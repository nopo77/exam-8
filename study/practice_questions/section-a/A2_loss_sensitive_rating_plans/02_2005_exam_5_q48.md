# 2005 Exam 5 - Q48 revised

**Points:** 2

## Question

Using the information below, calculate the premium for a $10,000 deductible policy. Show all work.

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| $250,000 | Standard Premium |
| 60% | Expected Loss Ratio |
| 15% | ALAE as a percentage of loss |
| 20% | Commission as a percentage of net premium |
| 10% | Other variable expenses |
| $15,000 | Fixed expenses |
| 0.25 | Loss Elimination Ratio at a $10,000 deductible |

- Assume that ALAE is not subject to the deductible.

## Solution

Our only option to get expected ground-up losses for the policy is to multiply the standard premium times the expected loss ratio.

**Expected Ground-up Losses:** $150,000 — `=B6*B7`

With a deductible, the losses eliminated from the insurer's perspective are the losses below the deductible. As such, the numerator of the loss elimination ratio with a deductible is expected losses below the deductible. Since the policy covers losses above the deductible, we multiply the expected ground-up losses by 1 minus the LER to get expected losses on the policy.

**Expected Losses on Policy:** $112,500 — `=D19*(1-B12)`

ALAE not being subject to the deductible means that the insurer will cover ALAE on a ground-up basis.

**ALAE:** $22,500 — `=B8*D19`

**Premium:** $214,286 — `=(D25+D29+B11)/(1-B9-B10)`
