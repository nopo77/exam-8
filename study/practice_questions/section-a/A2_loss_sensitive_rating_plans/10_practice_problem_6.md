# Practice Problem 6 - Fisher Chapter 2 Q10

## Question

In what way is a loss-sensitive dividend plan unbalanced?

## Solution

A loss-sensitive dividend plan is unbalanced because if loss experience is better than expected, the insured receives a dividend, but if loss experience is worse than expected, the insured does not incur any additional costs.
