# Practice Problem 1

## Question

Briefly discuss several advantages to insureds of a loss-sensitive rating plan.

## Solution

Advantages include:

- A financial incentive for loss control
- Opportunity to save money in the short-term with good experience
- Possible cash-flow benefits compared to a traditional policy
- Possible savings from reduced premium-based taxes and assessments
