# Practice Problem 13

## Question

Discuss how Lasso Credibility aligns with ASOP 25: Credibility Procedures.

## Solution

Lasso Credibility meets ASOP 25's definition of a credibility procedure as applied to the risk characteristics in the model, which involves blending their subject experience with identified relevant experience. In this framework, the subject experience is the relativity indicated by the modeling data, while the relevant experience is the complement of credibility provided by the actuary through the model's offset term. The penalized regression process itself, which uses a penalty parameter to balance the two, serves as the method for blending the data. This approach adheres to the standard's principles, requiring the actuary to use care in selecting the complement and to appropriately consider the characteristics of both data sources through the tuning of the penalty parameter.
