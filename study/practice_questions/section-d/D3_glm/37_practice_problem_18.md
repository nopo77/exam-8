# Practice Problem 18

## Question

Briefly describe deviance residuals.

## Solution

The deviance residual squared is the amount that a given observation contributes to the unscaled deviance. It is effectively the residual adjusted for the shape of the GLM distribution, so the deviance residual distribution will be normally distributed if the assumed GLM distribution is correct.
