# Practice Problem 3

## Question

In their paper, Couret & Venter discuss the use of a Holdout Sample.

### Part a

What is the purpose of a Holdout Sample?

### Part b

Name the way Couret & Venter produced an unbiased Holdout Sample from their original data.

## Solution

### Part a

A Holdout Sample is used to test the predictive ability of a model built on data excluding the holdout sample data. If the model does not do a good job of predicting the holdout sample results, it is likely that the model has been overfit to the sample data used in building the model (or that the model has poor predictive power in general).

### Part b

They used even years in the modeling data and odd years as the holdout sample. That helped neutralize for differences in development and trend between datasets.
