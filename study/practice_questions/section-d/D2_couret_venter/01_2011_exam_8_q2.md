# 2011 Exam 8 - Q2

**Points:** 1.5

## Question

A multi-dimensional credibility technique has been developed to predict claim frequencies for major permanent partial claims.

- Seven years of data were collected.
- The technique produced a raw predicted relativity based on the oldest five years.
- The most recent two years were used as the holdout sample.

Major Permanent Partial Claims

| Quintile | Holdout Sample Relativity | Prediction Based on Raw | Prediction Based on Credibility Procedure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0.6 | 0.3 | 0.4 |
| 2 | 0.8 | 0.5 | 0.7 |
| 3 | 1 | 1.1 | 1.0 |
| 4 | 1.2 | 1.9 | 1.5 |
| 5 | 1.4 | 3.0 | 1.8 |

Demonstrate whether the credibility technique produces an improved estimate using the sum of squared errors.

## Solution

The optimal method for predicting claim frequency will be the one that produces the lowest Sum of Squared Errors. For the raw data, the numbers given are X_quintile / X_h; for the credibility procedure, the numbers are x^est_quintile / x^est_h; for the holdout sample, the numbers are X_quintile,holdout / X_h,holdout. Note the use of "x" instead of "v" since this problem deals with Major PP claims instead of Fatal claims.
