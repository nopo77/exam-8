# Practice Problem 6 - Bahnemann Problem 6.3

## Question

Given the following information for a products liability policy:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| $13,000 | Policy premium |
| $2,100,000 | Insured's exposure amount (product sales) |
| $1,000 | Exposure unit (sales) |
| 9,950 | Severity at the policy limit |
| 30% | Variable expense |
| $0.50 | Fixed expense per exposure unit |

Calculate:

(a) rate R.

(b) pure premium p.

(c) claim frequency φ.

(d) expected # policy claims E[N].

(e) policy expected loss.

## Solution

### Part a

**R:** $6.19 — `=B6/(B7/B8)`

### Part b

**p:** $3.83 — `=(1-B10)*C22-B11`

### Part c

**freq:** 0.00039 — `=C24/B9`

### Part d

**E[N]:** 0.809 — `=C26*(B7/B8)`

### Part e

**E[Loss]:** 8,050 — `=C28*B9`
