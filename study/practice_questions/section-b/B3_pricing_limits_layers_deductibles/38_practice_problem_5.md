# Practice Problem 5 - Bahnemann Problem 6.2

## Question

Given the following information for a products liability policy:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| $13,000 | Policy premium |
| $2,100,000 | Insured's exposure amount (product sales) |
| $1,000 | Exposure unit (sales) |
| 9,950 | Severity at the policy limit |
| 1.60 | Policy loss-cost multiplier |

Calculate:

(a) rate R.

(b) pure premium p.

(c) claim frequency φ.

(d) expected # policy claims E[N].

(e) policy expected loss.

(f) variable expense ratio v.

## Solution

### Part a

**R:** $6.19 — `=B6/(B7/B8)`

### Part b

**p:** $3.87 — `=C22/B10`

### Part c

**Freq:** 0.00039 — `=C24/B9`

### Part d

**E[N]:** 0.817 — `=C26*(B7/B8)`

### Part e

**E[Loss]:** 8,125 — `=C28*B9`

### Part f

**v:** 37.5% — `=1-1/B10`
