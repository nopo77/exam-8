# 2003 Exam 9 - Q37

**Points:** 2

## Question

Using the information below, what is the pure premium for a $1,000,000 basic limits policy?

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| 0.10 | Frequency |
| $130,000 | Severity of $2,000,000 policy |
| 1.75 | Increased Limit Factor of $5,000,000 limit |
| $5,000 | Pure Premium of $3,000,000 excess of $2,000,000 policy |

## Solution

**pure prem at 2M:** $13,000.00 — `=B7*B6`

**pp at 5M:** $18,000.00 — `=D12+B9`

**pp at 1M:** $10,285.71 — `=D14/B8`
