# Practice Problem 4 - Bahnemann Problem 6.1

## Question

For a certain liability coverage:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| 0.00075 | φ, the claim frequency |
| 6,000 | E[Y], the claim severity |
| 25% | Variable expense provision. All expenses are variable. |
| 2,500 | Exposure units for insured |

Calculate:

(a) pure premium p.

(b) expected # policy claims E[N].

(c) policy expected loss.

(d) loss-cost multiplier Ψ.

(e) rate R.

(f) policy premium P.

## Solution

### Part a

**p:** 4.5 — `=B6*B7`

### Part b

**E[N]:** 1.875 — `=B6*B9`

### Part c

**E[Loss]:** 11,250 — `=C21*B9`

### Part d

**LCM:** 1.333 — `=1/(1-B8)`

### Part e

**R:** 6 — `=C21*C27`

### Part f

**P:** 15,000 — `=C29*B9`
