# Practice Problem 13 - Fisher Chapter 3 Q12

## Question

You price a retrospective policy with the following information:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| $150,000 | Expected loss |
| $300,000 | Aggregate limit |

You find that the insurance charge is:

$15,000 — Insurance charge

The customer requests that you also add a per-occurrence loss limitation to the losses subject to the retrospective calculation.

$100,000 — Per-occurrence loss limit

You determine that if there were no aggregate limit, the cost of the per-occurrence limit would be:

$50,000 — Per-occurrence limit cost without aggregate limit

Would the combined charge for the per-occurrence limit and the aggregate limit be more, less, or the same as the sum of the two charges, $65,000? Why?

## Solution

The combined charge would be less because it would consider the overlap between the per-occurrence limit and the aggregate limit.
