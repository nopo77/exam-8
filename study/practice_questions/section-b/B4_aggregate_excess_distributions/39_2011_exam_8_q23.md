# 2011 Exam 8 - Q23

**Points:** 2

## Question

An actuary calculated the aggregate loss for a policy using the following:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| $250,000 | Expected total loss |
| $100,000 | Deductible |
| 40% | Percentage of total loss excess of $100,000 |
| $300,000 | Aggregate limit |

The following table provides insurance charges contemplating the indicated deductibles:

Deductible

| B | C | D | E | F |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entry Ratio | $100,000 | $200,000 | $300,000 | $400,000 |
| 1.0 | 0.430 | 0.460 | 0.475 | 0.487 |
| 1.5 | 0.280 | 0.330 | 0.350 | 0.360 |
| 2.0 | 0.170 | 0.235 | 0.260 | 0.275 |
| 2.5 | 0.105 | 0.165 | 0.195 | 0.205 |
| 3.0 | 0.065 | 0.115 | 0.150 | 0.170 |

The actuary later learns that the expected total loss should have been:

$333,000 — Corrected expected total loss

Calculate the dollar difference between the corrected expected insurance charge and the insurance charge that was used to price the policy.

## Solution

Using corrected loss:

**E[A_D]:** $199,800 — `=B23*(1-B8)`

**r*_G:** 1.50 — `=B9/L4`

Charge — 0.280 — `=C16` — $55,944 — `=L8*L4`

Using incorrect loss:

**E[A_D]:** $150,000 — `=B6*(1-B8)`

**r*_G:** 2 — `=B9/L12`

Charge — 0.170 — `=C17` — $25,500 — `=L16*L12`

**Dollar difference:** $30,444 — `=M8-M16`
