## Question

| Risk | Aggregate Loss Ratio |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 18% |
| 2 | 18% |
| 3 | 60% |
| 4 | 60% |
| 5 | 144% |

r

0.00

0.30

0.60

1.00

1.50

2.40
