# 2010 Exam 9 - Q28

**Points:** 3

## Question

An insurer writes five identical workers compensation risks for one year and observes the following:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| $1,250,000 | Standard Premium for Each Risk |
| 6% | General Overhead Expense as a Percentage of Standard Premium |
| 4.5% | Credit Risk Charge as a Percentage of Standard Premium |
| 11% | Expected ULAE as a Percentage of Loss and ALAE |
| 4% | Loss Based Assessment as a Percentage of Loss and ALAE |
| 10% | Excess Loss Pure Premium Factor for a $250,000 Deductible as a |

Percentage of Loss and ALAE

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| 8% | Acquisition Expense as a Percentage of Standard Premium |
| 3% | Premium Tax as a Percentage of Standard Premium |
| 5% | Profit Load as a Percentage of Net Premium |

Actual losses for each risk are as follows:

| Risk | Actual Loss + ALAE |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | $400,000 |
| 2 | $800,000 |
| 3 | $1,000,000 |
| 4 | $1,200,000 |
| 5 | $1,600,000 |

### Part a (2.00 pts)

Construct a table M for this book of business by displaying the insurance charges and savings for entry ratios from 0.00 to 1.60 in increments of 0.20.

### Part b (1.00 pts)

For one of the risks above, calculate a prospective premium for a large dollar deductible (LDD) policy with a per-occurrence deductible of $250,000 and an annual aggregate deductible (AAD) of $1,260,000.
