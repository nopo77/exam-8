# 2010 Exam 9 - Q29

**Points:** 2.5

## Question

The following information applies to a retrospectively rated policy:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| $25,000 | Standard Premium |
| $23,125 | Guaranteed Cost Premium |
| $21,000 | Provision for Losses and Expenses excluding Taxes |
| $12,500 | Expected Losses |
| 1.20 | Loss Conversion Factor |
| 0.050 | Charge for Maximum |
| 0.550 | Savings for Maximum |
| 0.683 | Charge for Minimum |
| 0.017 | Savings for Minimum |

### Part a (0.50 pts)

Determine the tax multiplier.

### Part b (0.50 pts)

Determine the minimum premium ratio as a percentage of standard premium.

### Part c (0.50 pts)

Calculate the converted net insurance charge in dollars.

### Part d (0.50 pts)

Determine the loss ratio at minimum.

## Solution

### Part a

The "Provision for Losses and Expenses excluding Taxes" is e + E[A], i.e., the expense provision + expected losses.

T — 1.10 — `=B7/B8` — GCP = (e + E[A])T

### Part b

Charge diff = (GCP - H)/(c*E[A]*T)

**Charge diff:** 0.633 — `=B13-B11`

**H:** $12,669 — `=B7-K8*B10*B9*J4`

**H/P:** 50.7% — `=J10/B6`

### Part c

**cI:** $495 — `=B10*(B11-B14)*B9`

### Part d

It wasn't really clear here whether they wanted the loss ratio to retrospective premium, or to standard premium. Since the CAS model solutions assumed standard premium, that's what I'll show here, though a ratio to the minimum premium calculated in part (b) should have also been a valid answer.

I assume that the loss ratio is being asked as a percentage of standard premium.

**r_H:** 0.334 — `=B14-B13+1`

**L_H:** $4,175 — `=J22*B9`

**LR at min:** 16.7% — `=J24/B6`

If you calculated the loss ratio at minimum relative to retrospective premium, it would be:

33.0% — `=J24/J10`
