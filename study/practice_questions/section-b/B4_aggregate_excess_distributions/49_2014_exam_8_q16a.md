# 2014 Exam 8 - Q16a

**Points:** 1.25

## Question

A company experiences an annual level of low-severity losses totaling $500,000 and periodic loss events as shown in the table below:

| Period of Occurrence | Descriptor | Loss Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Once every Five years | Additional Low Severity Losses | $2,000,000 |
| Once every Three years | Single Large Loss | $1,000,000 |

All loss events are independent of each other.

The company and their insurer agree on a Large Dollar Deductible (LDD) policy with the following characteristics:

- minimizes effect of a single large loss
- guarantees reimbursable loss will not exceed $2,000,000
- results in expected annual reimbursable loss of $1,000,000

Design an LDD plan that meets the goals of the company. Note that the expected losses can be expressed as a function of the aggregate maximum and the per occurrence limit.

## Solution

The reimbursable loss is the amount that the insured will have to reimburse the insurer, since with an LDD policy, the insurer will pay all claims on a ground-up basis first and then seek reimbursement from the insured. This will include all losses below the occurrence deductible, limited by the aggregate limit on deductible losses.

The aggregate deductible should be $2M, equal to the maximum reimbursable loss

Let occurrence deductible = X

If the 1 in 3 year large loss occurs, assume it will be limited by X. In other words, assume X is less than $1M.

| Prob | Unlim Loss | Occ Lim Loss | Occ & Agg Lim Loss | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.53 | $500,000 | $500,000 | $500,000 | No extra losses occur |
| 0.27 | $1,500,000 | 500000+X | 500000+X | Only single large loss occurs, limited by X |
| 0.13 | $2,500,000 | $2,500,000 | $2,000,000 | Only additional low sev losses occur |
| 0.07 | $3,500,000 | 2500000+X | $2,000,000 | Both large loss (lim by X) and additional losses occur |

<details><summary>Formulas</summary>

- `H15` = `=(1-1/3)*(1-1/5)`
- `K15` = `=MIN(J15,2000000)`
- `H16` = `=(1/3)*(1-1/5)`
- `I16` = `=I15+E9`
- `H17` = `=(1-1/3)*(1/5)`
- `I17` = `=I15+E8`
- `K17` = `=MIN(J17,2000000)`
- `H18` = `=(1/3)*(1/5)`
- `I18` = `=I15+E8+E9`

</details>

E[Occ & Agg Lim Loss] = 1M = — $666,667 — `=H15*K15+H17*K17+H18*K18` — +.27*(500000+X)

**Solve for X:** $750,000 — `=(1000000-J20)/H16-500000`

So the plan should have an occurrence deductible of $750k and an aggregate deductible of $2M.
