# 2006 Exam 9 - Q32

**Points:** 4

## Question

A retrospectively rated workers compensation policy has the following parameters:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| 0.96 | Minimum premium factor |
| 0.30 | Basic premium factor |
| 0.60 | Expected loss ratio |
| 0.40 | Expense ratio |
| 1.05 | Loss conversion factor |
| 1.00 | Tax multiplier |

The insurance charge at entry ratio r is given by the formula:

X(r) = 0.8 / (0.8 + r)

Calculate the maximum premium factor for this policy.

## Solution

The steps in this solution are:

1. Get r_H

2. Get the charge at r_H

3. Get the charge at r_G

4. Get r_G

5. Get G/P

H/P = (B/P + c(r_HE[A]/P))T

| H | I |
| --- | --- |
| Solve for r_H | 1.048 |
| Charge at r_H | 0.433 |

<details><summary>Formulas</summary>

- `I11` = `=((B6/B11)-B7)/(B10*B8)`
- `I12` = `=0.8/(0.8+I11)`

</details>

From here, we can either calculate I from the basic premium formula (assuming a balanced plan, as usual), or we can use the charge difference balance equation to solve for the charge at r_G.

Solution 1: Calculate I to get the charge at r_G

B/P = e/P - (c-1)E[A]/P + cI/P

**Solve for I/P:** -0.067 — `=(B7-B9+(B10-1)*B8)/B10`

I/P = (Charge(r_G) - Savings(r_H))E[A]/P

**Savings r_H:** 0.481 — `=I12+I11-1`

**Solve for Charge(r_G):** 0.369 — `=(I21/B8)+I25`

**r_G:** 1.365 — `=0.8/I27-0.8`

**G/P:** 1.16 — `=(B7+B10*I29*B8)*B11`

Solution 2: Calculate the charge difference balance equation to get the charge at r_G

Charge(r_H) - Charge(r_G) = [(e/P + E[A]/P)T - H/P]/[c(E[A]/P)T]

| M | O |
| --- | --- |
| Charge difference | 0.063 |
| Charge at r_G | 0.369 |

<details><summary>Formulas</summary>

- `O21` = `=((B9+B8)*B11-B6)/(B10*B8*B11)`
- `O22` = `=I12-O21`

</details>

**r_G:** 1.365 — `=0.8/O22-0.8`

**G/P:** 1.16 — `=(B7+B10*O24*B8)*B11`
