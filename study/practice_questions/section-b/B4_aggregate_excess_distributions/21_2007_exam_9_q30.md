# 2007 Exam 9 - Q30

**Points:** 2

## Question

An actuary is pricing a retrospectively rated policy for which

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| $40,000 | Aggregate loss at minimum premium |
| $60,000 | Aggregate loss at maximum premium |

The actuary has decided to base the price of this policy on the following aggregate losses, experienced by 10 policies that are identical to the policy being priced.

| Policy Number | Aggregate Losses |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | $5,000 |
| 2 | $10,000 |
| 3 | $20,000 |
| 4 | $40,000 |
| 5 | $40,000 |
| 6 | $50,000 |
| 7 | $60,000 |
| 8 | $75,000 |
| 9 | $80,000 |
| 10 | $120,000 |

Calculate this policy's net insurance charge as a percent of expected aggregate loss.

## Solution

There are many ways to solve this. I'll show several.

Solution 1: Using vertical slices

| H | I | J |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charge | $9,500 | E[max(0,A-60000)] |
| Savings | $8,500 | E[max(0,40000-A)] |

<details><summary>Formulas</summary>

- `I6` = `=(SUM(C20:C22)-60000*3)/10`
- `I7` = `=(40000*3-SUM(C13:C15))/10`

</details>

**I/E[A]:** 2.0% — `=(I6-I7)/AVERAGE(C13:C22)`
