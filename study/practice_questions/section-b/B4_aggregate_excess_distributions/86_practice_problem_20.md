# Practice Problem 20 - Fisher Chapter 4 Q1

## Question

Would you expect a fair premium for a retrospective plan with a high aggregate loss limit to be more or less than the fair premium for a guaranteed cost plan covering the same risk?

## Solution

The fair premium should be lower, as the insured is responsible for a significant fraction of the risk.
