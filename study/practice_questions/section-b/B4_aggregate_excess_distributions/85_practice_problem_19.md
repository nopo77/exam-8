# Practice Problem 19 - Fisher Chapter 3 Q19

## Question

When all else is equal, if the variance of the loss distribution is larger, will the Table M charge be larger or smaller than with a smaller variance?

## Solution

The Table M charge for a given entry ratio will be larger when the variance of the loss distribution is larger, all else being equal.
