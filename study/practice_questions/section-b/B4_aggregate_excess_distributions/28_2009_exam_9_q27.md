# 2009 Exam 9 - Q27

**Points:** 5

## Question

An insured has purchased a large dollar deductible workers compensation policy providing reimbursement up to $250,000 per accident, subject to an annual limit of $1,400,000.

The following characteristics apply to the policy:

| B | C |
| --- | --- |
| $800,000 | Expected unlimited losses |
| 1.45 | Loss group adjustment factor |
| 0.90 | State and hazard group differential |

The following additional information is also available:

| --- | --- |
| 20 | 750,001 - 850,000 |
| 19 | 850,001 - 1,000,000 |
| 18 | 1,000,001 - 1,250,000 |
| 17 | 1,250,001 - 1,500,000 |
| 16 | 1,500,001 - 1,750,000 |
| 15 | 1,750,001 - 2,000,000 |

Expected Loss Group

| C | D | E | F | G | H | I |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 2.51 - 2.75 | 0.182 | 0.241 | 0.261 | 0.254 | 0.252 | 0.247 |

It is later determined that the expected unlimited losses are $1,000,000. Calculate the percentage error in the expected losses to be paid by the insurer under this policy.

## Solution

The loss group adjustment factor is a reference to the ICRLL procedure. So clearly the intention is that the table given is a regular Table M.

1.45 = (1+.8k)/(1-k)

**k:** 0.20 — `=(B10-1)/(B10+0.8)`

Original estimate:

| --- | --- | --- |
| E[A] - E[A_D] | $160,000 |  |
| E[A_D] | $640,000 |  |
| Adj Exp Loss | $1,044,000 |  |
| Lookup AEL | ELG | 18 |
