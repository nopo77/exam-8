# 2009 Exam 9 - Q32

**Points:** 4

## Question

The unlimited and limited loss ratios for five risks of equal size are as follows:

| Risk # | Unlimited Loss Ratio | Limited Loss Ratio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 25% | 25% |
| 2 | 50% | 50% |
| 3 | 75% | 75% |
| 4 | 100% | 75% |
| 5 | 100% | 100% |

### Part a (0.50 pts)

Calculate the loss elimination ratio.

### Part b (1.50 pts)

Calculate the Table M charges at loss ratios from 0% to 100% using increments of 25%.

### Part c (2.00 pts)

Calculate the Table L charges at loss ratios from 0% to 100% using increments of 25%.

## Solution

### Part a

| I | K |
| --- | --- |
| E[A]/P | 70% |
| E[A_D]/P | 65% |

<details><summary>Formulas</summary>

- `K2` = `=AVERAGE(C7:C11)`
- `K3` = `=AVERAGE(D7:D11)`

</details>

LER — 7.14% — `=1-K3/K2` — This is for a limit, and consistent with what we'll use for Table L in part (c).
